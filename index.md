Privacy Policy

This application uses the TikTok API to automate the process of uploading videos on behalf of the user. 
We do not collect, store, or share personal information except for the authentication data required 
by TikTok to authorize the app.

Data collected:
- TikTok access tokens required for authentication
- Basic account information allowed through TikTok’s OAuth permissions

Purpose:
The data is used strictly to enable video uploads and content publishing through n8n automation.

Data storage:
Authentication tokens are securely stored by n8n and are not shared with third parties.

User rights:
You may revoke the app’s access at any time from your TikTok account settings.

Contact:
For questions or issues related to this privacy policy, please contact the application owner.
